01 The idea

The web is a place.
Its frame should feel like one.

Prism explores a different kind of Chromium browser: one that feels at home on macOS, gives open tabs room to breathe, and keeps the tools of everyday browsing close at hand.

03 Built in the open

Curious by nature.
Honest by design.

Prism is an evolving personal project, shared under GPL-3.0. It’s a macOS prototype today, and its edges are part of the work. Performance depends on your sites and workload; no measured comparison with Arc is published.
